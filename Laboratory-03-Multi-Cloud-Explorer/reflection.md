# Reflection

## My Reflection

This laboratory activity helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud all provide core services for computing, storage, networking, and identity, but each platform has different strengths and is suited to different business requirements.

I learned that AWS is known for its broad service portfolio and global infrastructure, Azure is especially useful for organizations that already depend on Microsoft technologies, and Google Cloud is particularly strong in artificial intelligence, data analytics, and Kubernetes. Comparing the providers helped me see why a cloud solutions architect must consider the client's existing technology, budget, technical requirements, scalability needs, and future goals before making a recommendation.

Overall, this activity improved my understanding of multi-cloud evaluation and showed me how cloud engineering decisions can be connected to real business requirements. In future cloud projects, I would compare services and costs carefully before choosing a provider instead of making a decision based only on popularity.
